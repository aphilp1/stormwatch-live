What is CAMS?
Convection-Allowing Models (CAMs) run at 3 km grid spacing or finer — fine enough to explicitly simulate individual thunderstorm updrafts and downdrafts. Coarser models (12–25 km) must parameterize convection using statistical rules; CAMs actually resolve it.

NOAA's National Severe Storms Lab (NSSL) runs several CAMs daily and posts side-by-side comparisons so forecasters can assess agreement and skill against real observations.
