MECHANISM VERDICT (Q1)

  SUPPORTED: HRRR under-mixes its own aloft wind to the surface in
  offshore events — the real atmosphere (obs/bc) achieves higher surface-
  to-850hPa ratios than HRRR does. This is consistent with the observed
  underbias pattern.

  Offshore (850 hPa) mean deficit: +0.144
  Continental (700 hPa) mean deficit: +0.015

  CAVEAT: bc_level is confounded with regime (850=offshore, 700=continental).
  The deficit difference could partly reflect the longer vertical mixing
  path at 700 hPa vs 850 hPa, independent of regime dynamics.
  kincade_run is the natural control: the one offshore event with near-zero
  bias also has near-zero deficit (obs_ratio ≈ hrrr_ratio), consistent
  with the mechanism — when HRRR gets the mixing right, the bias disappears.
